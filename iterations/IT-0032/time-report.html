6. What was changed

  1. The ticket table is replaced by a responsive grid of cards, styled like the Grid View vehicle cards of the same application (coloured top edge by priority, status badge, field panel, footer with time and actions).

  3. Kept unchanged: status tabs, search, priority and category filters, paging (10/20/50), selecting a ticket to open its details, edit, delete, new ticket.
